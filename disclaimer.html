AI-specific note

AI can sound certain while being wrong

AI systems can hallucinate, omit context, invent sources, misunderstand prompts, overfit examples, miss edge cases, and produce convincing but flawed output. Treat AI output as something to review, not something to automatically trust. When consequences matter, slow down and verify.

  • Check facts. Names, dates, numbers, links, claims, and source quality can drift or be wrong.
  • Check fit. A workflow that is safe for a draft may be unsafe for code, policy, customer data, infrastructure, or a decision record.
  • Check authority. A guide can explain concepts, but it cannot grant permission, override policy, or replace approval.

Field guide takeaway: These pages are a map, not a permission slip. Read them, verify them, adapt them to your context, and keep human judgement on the controls before the work touches people, systems, data, money, or public communication.
