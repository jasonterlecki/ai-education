AI Knowledge Field Guide

AI Education Knowledge Base

A practical field guide for using AI safely, creatively, and accountably, from everyday prompting through structured workflows, technical agent work, and enterprise operating models.

Entry point: Beginner friendly Audience: Business, technical, and governance teams Version: 1.7

The core rule

Start with the lowest-risk guide that matches the work. Use Beginner pages for everyday AI literacy, Intermediate pages when AI output becomes workflow direction, Advanced pages when code or systems are involved, and Expert pages when policy, architecture, or operating model decisions are on the table.

AI can draft, explore, compare, summarize, and inspect. People still own the facts, the decision, the approval, the risk, and the result.

Education ladder

The higher the blast radius, the stronger the controls. Let the page level tell you how much review gravity is needed.

1 BeginnerDrafting, summarizing, safe prompting, and output review.
2 IntermediateReusable workflows, vibe briefs, planning, and checkpoints.
3 AdvancedCode, infrastructure, security, tests, automation, and rollback.
4 ExpertStrategy, governance, architecture, portfolio, and operating model.
1

Vibe

Explore shape, tone, options, and direction while the cost of being wrong is still low.

2

Plan

Convert the chosen direction into scope, non-goals, phases, validation, rollout, and rollback.

3

Checkpoint

Execute one bounded slice, validate it, report what changed, and stop before the next slice.

4

Review

Verify facts, diffs, permissions, tests, risk, ownership, and recovery before moving forward.

When to climb levels

Move up the ladder when the AI output stops being a draft and starts becoming a decision, workflow, system change, or operational dependency.

From Beginner to IntermediateThe output will guide repeated work or influence how others act.
From Intermediate to AdvancedThe work touches code, data, infrastructure, security, automation, or production-adjacent systems.
From Advanced to ExpertThe choice affects policy, architecture, access, evaluation, governance, or enterprise adoption.

Field guide takeaway

Use AI with creative energy and operational brakes. Vibe when you are exploring, plan when the work becomes real, checkpoint when agents execute, and review before the blast radius gets teeth.

Good useAI accelerates thinking, drafting, comparison, review, and controlled implementation.
Risky useAI becomes a quiet approval path for facts, access, code, infrastructure, data, or public communication.
