AI Education Field Guide

A practical guide to using AI safely and effectively, from everyday literacy through structured workflows, technical agent work, and enterprise governance.

Expertise: Beginner entry point Audience: Business and technical teams Use when: You need role-appropriate AI guidance Version: 1.1

Purpose

This repository helps people understand AI without treating it as magic or as a replacement for accountable human judgment. Start with the Beginner guides for safe everyday use, then move into more structured and technical material as the risk and responsibility increase.
