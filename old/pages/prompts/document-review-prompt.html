Document Review Prompt

Use AI to find unclear structure, missing context, inconsistent tone, unsupported claims, and practical risks.

Expertise: Beginner Category: Prompt Library Audience: Document authors and reviewers Use when: Reviewing a draft before sharing Version: 1.0.1
Back to index Previous: Email and Communication Next: Executive Summary

Who this is for

This prompt is for people reviewing reports, memos, proposals, instructions, policies, or internal drafts for clarity and risk.

What you will learn

  • How to ask AI for a structured document review.
  • How to separate clarity feedback from unsupported claims.
  • How to keep sensitive content out of unapproved tools.

When to use this

Use it before sharing a draft when you want a second-pass review for clarity, completeness, audience fit, and actionability.

When not to use this

Do not paste confidential contracts, customer records, regulated data, privileged material, or sensitive incidents into an unapproved AI tool.

Good use and risky use

Good use

Use AI to find unclear sections, missing context, contradictions, and claims that need verification before a person approves the document.

Risky use

Paste sensitive documents into unapproved tools or accept AI edits that change meaning, policy, obligations, or risk.

Copy-ready prompt template

Document review 
Review this document for:
- clarity
- structure
- missing sections
- contradictions
- unsupported claims
- audience fit
- tone
- actionability
- risks
- suggested edits

Audience:
[AUDIENCE]

Purpose:
[PURPOSE]

Document:
[PASTE REDACTED DOCUMENT OR SUMMARY]

Return:
1. Overall assessment
2. Highest-priority fixes
3. Missing or unclear information
4. Unsupported claims to verify
5. Suggested edits by section
6. Questions for the author
Safer alternative: If the document is sensitive, provide a summary, structure, and anonymized excerpts instead of the full text.

How to adapt it

Audience: Tell AI who will read the document and what they need from it. Purpose: State whether the document informs, recommends, requests approval, or gives instructions. Depth: Ask for light edit, structural review, risk review, or executive polish. Evidence: Ask AI to separate verified facts from claims that need checking. Sections: Ask for section-by-section feedback if the document is long. Output: Request prioritized edits instead of a full rewrite when review is the goal.

Review before using the output

Claims: Verify unsupported claims before keeping them. Meaning: Confirm suggested edits do not change approved intent. Risk: Escalate legal, HR, financial, security, or compliance concerns. Privacy: Remove sensitive source text and output. Audience: Check that the final draft fits reader needs. Completeness: Confirm required sections and decisions are present.

Key takeaway

AI is useful for review questions and clarity checks, but document owners must verify facts, intent, risk, and final wording.
