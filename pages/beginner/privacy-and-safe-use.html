Privacy and Safe Use

Protect people, customers, credentials, and confidential work by keeping sensitive details out of unapproved AI tools.

Expertise: Beginner Audience: General business users Use when: You are deciding what can go into an AI prompt Version: 1.0.1
Back to index Previous: Prompting Basics Next: Hallucinations and Verification

Who this is for

This guide is for anyone using AI with workplace information, documents, notes, messages, or examples.

What you will learn

  • What not to paste into AI tools.
  • Safer ways to ask for help.
  • When to use approved tools or official review.

Do not paste sensitive details

Before using AI, assume anything you paste may need to be protected unless your organization has approved the tool and the data type. Remove sensitive details first.

Never paste: passwords, API keys, tokens, private certificates, or access codes. Do not paste: customer data, employee personal data, medical data, regulated records, or account details. Do not paste: confidential contracts, internal incidents, unreleased financials, private strategy, or privileged material.

Good use and risky use

Good use

Redact names, replace private details with placeholders, summarize the situation at a high level, and use approved AI tools for approved data types.

Risky use

Paste a full contract, customer email, incident report, spreadsheet, credential, or unreleased business result into an AI tool without approval.

Try this workflow

Use a safer placeholder version of your request.

1. Remove names, account numbers, credentials, private dates, and exact financial details. 2. Replace sensitive details with labels like [customer], [contract type], [system], or [amount range]. 3. Ask for general structure, wording, checklist, or questions to consider. 4. Review the answer yourself before adding real details in approved systems.

Key takeaway

If the information would be harmful, confidential, regulated, or embarrassing if exposed, do not paste it into an AI tool unless the tool and data use are explicitly approved.
