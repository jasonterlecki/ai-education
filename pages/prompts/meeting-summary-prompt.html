Meeting Summary Prompt

The best meeting summary preserves decisions and accountability.

Expertise: Beginner Also useful for: Intermediate Category: Prompt Library Audience: Meeting owners and project teams Version: 1.0
Back to index Previous: Executive Summary Next: Vibe Brief

Who this is for

This prompt is for people turning meeting notes, agendas, or approved transcripts into minutes, actions, and follow-ups.

When to use this

Use it after a meeting when you need a clear summary, decisions made, action items, owners, due dates, and unresolved questions.

When not to use this

Do not paste confidential transcript material, HR discussions, sensitive customer details, regulated data, or privileged conversations into unapproved AI tools.

Copy-ready prompt template

Meeting minutes 
Turn these meeting notes into useful minutes.

Meeting purpose:
[PURPOSE]

Attendees or roles:
[ATTENDEES OR ROLES]

Notes:
[PASTE REDACTED NOTES]

Return:
1. Brief summary
2. Decisions made
3. Action items with owners
4. Due dates
5. Unresolved questions
6. Risks or blockers
7. Suggested follow-up messages

If an owner, due date, or decision is unclear, mark it as "needs confirmation" instead of inventing it.
Confidentiality check: Use approved tools for transcripts or sensitive meetings. Redact names and details when they are not needed for the summary.

How to adapt it

Purpose: State why the meeting happened. Roles: Use roles instead of names when privacy matters. Actions: Ask for owner, due date, dependency, and status. Decisions: Separate decisions from discussion points. Follow-up: Request a short message for attendees if needed. Gaps: Require "needs confirmation" for unclear items.

Review before using the output

Decisions: Confirm that decisions were actually made. Owners: Check owners and due dates before sending. Privacy: Remove sensitive transcript material. Accuracy: Correct misunderstood context or names. Follow-up: Ensure messages are appropriate for recipients. Recordkeeping: Follow official meeting record rules when they apply.

Key takeaway

A strong meeting summary captures decisions, owners, due dates, and open questions without inventing certainty.
