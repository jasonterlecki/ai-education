Prompting Basics Template

A reusable structure for clear, safe prompts with role, goal, context, constraints, examples, output format, and review criteria.

Who this is for

This prompt is for anyone who wants better AI output without needing technical language or complex instructions.

When to use this

Use this for drafts, summaries, plans, explanations, checklists, analysis, and other everyday work where context and review matter.

When not to use this

Do not use it to paste secrets, passwords, API keys, regulated data, customer records, confidential contracts, or sensitive internal incidents into unapproved AI tools.

Copy-ready prompt template

General structure 
Act as [ROLE].
Your goal is to [GOAL].
Here is the context:
[CONTEXT]

Constraints:
- [CONSTRAINT 1]
- [CONSTRAINT 2]
- [CONSTRAINT 3]

Examples:
[OPTIONAL EXAMPLES OF THE STYLE, FORMAT, OR KIND OF ANSWER YOU WANT]

Use this style:
[STYLE / TONE]

Return the output as:
[FORMAT]

Before finalizing, check for:
- unsupported assumptions
- missing information
- unclear wording
- risks or limitations
Use placeholders. Replace sensitive names, customer details, credentials, financials, and private facts with generic labels before using an AI tool.

How to adapt it

Role: Name the perspective you want, such as reviewer, editor, analyst, or coach. Goal: Say what success looks like in one sentence. Context: Include only the information the tool needs, with sensitive details removed. Constraints: Add rules about tone, length, sources, privacy, and what not to change. Examples: Provide a short sample if format or style matters. Output: Ask for bullets, table, draft email, decision memo, checklist, or another reviewable format.

Review before using the output

Facts: Verify dates, names, numbers, sources, and claims. Privacy: Remove sensitive data before prompting and before sharing output. Fit: Check whether the output matches the audience and purpose. Assumptions: Look for invented details or unsupported conclusions. Ownership: Edit and approve the final result yourself. Escalation: Use official review for legal, HR, finance, security, medical, or compliance topics.

Key takeaway

A good prompt gives direction, context, boundaries, and review criteria. AI can draft the output, but people still own the result.
